Why daffodils?

When William Wordsworth stumbled upon a field of golden daffodils, he was wandering — lonely, lost, and without direction. But that unexpected burst of beauty stayed with him, filling his heart with joy long after the moment had passed.

That is the feeling we want to create. That poem was my (the founder's) favorite growing up, and it helped me in a time when I felt helpless. For every woman who feels lost, for every child who needs warmth — we want to be that unexpected field of daffodils. A moment of beauty and hope that changes everything.

"For oft, when on my couch I lie
in vacant or in pensive mood,
they flash upon that inward eye
which is the bliss of solitude." — William Wordsworth
U

Urvee

Founder, The Daffodil Foundation

A student from Wake Forest, NC with a heart for community. Urvee founded The Daffodil Foundation to bridge her love of health sciences with direct service to women and children in need, starting small, dreaming big.

What we do

Programs built
with heart.

01

Community drives

We organize regular drives to collect and distribute essential goods to women and children in need: hygiene products, clothing, food, and care packages filled with warmth and dignity.

  • Hygiene & toiletry drives
  • Period product distribution
  • Seasonal care packages
  • Back-to-school supply drives
02

Health & wellness workshops

Rooted in our founder's science and volunteering background, our workshops bring accessible health education and screenings directly to underserved women and families.

  • Women's health screenings
  • Nutrition & wellness education
  • Mental health awareness
  • First aid & safety skills
03

Education & mentorship

We believe education is one of the most powerful tools for lasting change. Our mentorship and workshop programs help young girls and women build skills, confidence, and hope for the future.

  • STEM mentorship for girls
  • Life skills workshops
  • College prep support
  • Literacy assistance
✦

Future: safe shelter

Our long-term vision is a full shelter for women and children. A warm, safe, dignified place to land. Every program we run today is a step toward making that dream real.
